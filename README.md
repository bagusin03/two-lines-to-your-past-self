The Honest Sentence: a two-line warning to my past self for the make.fun "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty. `main.ts` writes exactly two lines (273 characters, one sentence each) to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else. Fully offline and deterministic: no network, no clock, no randomness, standard library only.

> Don't tell Doctor Hartono "I'm fine" when he asks twice on the morning of 14 April 2024 — tell him the pain started on Sunday.
> That one honest sentence got you the scan that caught the tumour before it could spread, and it is why you never round your symptoms down anymore.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
